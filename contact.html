Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our chiropractic procedures, session plans, and clinic visits.

Chiropractic adjustments performed by trained, experienced specialists like Dr. Bharath Adla are exceptionally safe, non-invasive, and gentle. Most patients feel instant relief and a sensation of lightness in their spine right after the adjustment. The "popping" sound you might hear is merely the release of microscopic nitrogen gas bubbles from the synovial fluid in your joints (cavitation), not bones grinding together.

Every spine is unique, but most patients experience substantial pain reduction within 3 to 5 sessions. Acute sprains or subluxations often resolve quickly, whereas chronic disc bulges or years of sciatica may require a structured program of 8 to 12 sessions combining spinal decompression, fascial therapy (FDM), and core postural stabilization exercises to prevent recurrence.

No referral is required. You can directly book an appointment by calling us, sending a WhatsApp message, or completing our online booking form. However, if you already have recent spinal X-rays, MRI scans, or surgical discharge summaries, please bring them along to your first consultation to help Dr. Bharath Adla and Dr. Roja Eepuri tailor your care.

We recommend wearing loose, comfortable athletic clothing (such as track pants, t-shirts, or yoga wear) that allows free range of movement and easy access to the area of pain (such as knees, shoulders, or spine). Please bring any medical reports, previous MRI scans, or prescriptions you have.
