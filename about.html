    Our Story, Philosophy & Medical Team

    Who We Are

    Healing Pain at the Root Cause, Not Just the Symptoms

    Founded with the vision of offering high-level non-surgical spine and musculoskeletal solutions, Dr. Renu's Chiropractic / Osteopathy & Physiotherapy Clinic has grown into Boduppal's most trusted rehabilitation destination.

    Too often, patients struggling with lumbar disc herniations, sciatica, cervical spondylosis, or severe knee arthritis are told that heavy painkillers or invasive surgeries are their only options. At our clinic, we operate on a completely different philosophy: the human body possesses remarkable innate restorative potential when biomechanical alignment and neurological pathways are properly restored.

    By fusing advanced Chiropractic High-Velocity Low-Amplitude (HVLA) adjustments, osteopathic fascial mobilization, the world-renowned Fascial Distortion Model (FDM), and individualized physical therapy exercise programs, our clinicians ensure long-lasting relief without dependence on pharmaceuticals.
